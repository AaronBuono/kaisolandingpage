      3. If you weren’t at work tomorrow, would your team know what to do?
Would your employees know what who is responsible for what, who can make decisions and who they should ask for help?
For example: job responsibilities, clear role descriptions, project ownership.

      4. Do you ensure your team follows the same steps when completing critical work in the business, or does each person do it their own way? 
Do you have standardised processes when quoting, ordering materials, checking completed work and dealing with complaints etc.?

      5. Do you keep records to show that critical work in your business has been completed properly?
For example: completed checklists, inspection reports, approvals, test results or customer sign-offs.


         6. Do you prepare for when things go wrong and plan how you would deal with it?
For example: project delays, mistakes, injuries, equipment failure, spills or unreliable suppliers.

         7. Are you familiar with all the laws and rules your business must follow? Do you have a way to follow and keep up to date with changes?

For example: workplace safety laws, licences, permits, building rules or requirements in customer contracts.

         8. Do you check and make sure that your workers have the right skills and training for their work?
Do you regularly make sure your team have valid licenses, appropriate qualifications, safety training etc. before they complete important or risky work?


            9. Do you check your suppliers and subcontractors have the adequate experience, appropriate licenses, and can complete their work properly and safely?
For example, checking their insurance, qualifications, references from their previous work or projects etc.

            10. Have you set business goals and have a plan to achieve those goals? Do you regularly review the progress towards those goals?
For example: a plan to reduce re-work, errors, finishing more projects on time, preventing injuries or receiving fewer customer complaints etc.

            11. When something goes wrong, do you try find out why to stop it from happening again?
For example: investigating customer complaints, injuries, near misses, faulty work or missed deadlines.

ISO 9001 Quality questions

               12. Do you make sure you & your customer agree on what ‘a good job’ looks like? 
Do you agree and record what the customer expects before work begins?
For example: agreed price, deadlines, drawings, specifications and the finished result.


                  13. Are there any formal checks to approve completed work before giving it to the customer?
Do you make sure the work is correct and meets the customer’s requirements?
For example: inspections, testing, supervisor approval or final quality checks.
